What this is. The same model published in the written analysis, ported to the browser so the assumptions can be argued with. Structural figures (revenue, NOI, DSCR, equity) match the exact rational model computed by agent-calc bit for bit; IRR and NPV differ by at most 1.9 × 10⁻⁸ relative — float64 rounding in the discounting chain, fractions of a cent on a million-dollar NPV.

What it still assumes. Ramp of 55% then 80% of plan in years one and two, 3% growth and cost inflation, 40% blended ordinary tax with the depreciation shield usable, exit at appreciated value with full recapture. Peak funding need adds nine months of pre-opening burn and the operating trough — it is not the same as the down payment. Revenue assumptions are judgement, not data.
